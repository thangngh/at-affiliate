War-News Anxiety Check

For students & young adults (15–25) feeling overwhelmed by war/conflict news. 5 quick questions. Private, no sign-up.

1. How do you feel?

How often do you check war/conflict news per day?

Does the news make it hard to sleep?

Do you feel helpless about what's happening?

Do you avoid friends/focus because of the news?

Do you feel physical stress (racing heart, tense)?
