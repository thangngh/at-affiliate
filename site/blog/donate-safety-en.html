How to spot charity donation scams (2025)

War and disaster appeals always bring out scammers. Here is how to protect yourself before sending money.

6 common scam signs

  • Fake "Red Cross" or big-org logos copied from the web.
  • Demanding gift cards — real charities never do this.
  • Only accepting crypto or personal wallets.
  • DMs promising "matched donations".
  • Shady shortened links, not the official domain.
  • "Ends today" pressure so you don't verify.
